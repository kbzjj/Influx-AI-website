A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Should I choose tools or services?+

Use Influx Tool when your team wants to manage creator marketing with data and AI. Choose Influx Services when you want our team to support planning and execution. We can also discuss both.

Which platforms can I explore?+

The current product covers TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X. TikTok, YouTube and Instagram use official API data.

Are all four capabilities included in Plus?+

Plus focuses on creator discovery and analysis. Campaign management, creator outreach, reports, and data and brand insights are not included in Plus. See Pricing for the current plan scope.

Pricing
How is a services project scoped?+

We start with your brand goals, target markets, budget and timing. The work, responsibilities and quote are agreed around your project needs.
